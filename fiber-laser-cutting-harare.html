Questions

Questions we get asked about this

What thickness can you laser cut?

On the DXTECH fiber laser: mild steel to 20 mm on oxygen, stainless to 10 mm on nitrogen, aluminium to 8 mm, and galvanised sheet to 4 mm. The bed is 3 000 × 1 500 mm and tolerance is ± 0.1 mm, repeating to ± 0.03 mm.

Can you cut stainless steel?

Yes. The DXTECH fiber laser cuts stainless to 10 mm on nitrogen, and the workshop folds it to 3 000 mm and fabricates it to drawing — handrails, balustrades and sheet work.

What file formats do you accept for cutting?

DXF, DWG, STEP and PDF.

Can I get a price without drawings?

Yes. Send the measurements and a photograph of the site, or a description of what you need. Where the job needs someone on site, a site visit is arranged within two working days, and a written quotation follows within the week.
