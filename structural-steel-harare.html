Structural steelwork

Structural steel fabrication and erection in Harare

Kingson Engineering fabricates structural steelwork at No. 1262 Tynwald Industries in Harare and erects it on site with its own mobile crane. Portal frames, columns, rafters and purlins, set out from your drawings and put up by the same company that made them.

To give you an accurate price quickly, please send:

Questions

Questions we get asked about this

How long does a steel structure take?

A portal frame programme runs 6–10 weeks from order. A written quotation comes within 5 working days of your enquiry, and a site visit is arranged within 2 working days where the job needs one.

How long does a quotation take?

Enquiries are acknowledged the same working day, and a written quotation follows within 5 working days. Where the job needs someone on site first, the site visit is arranged within 2 working days.

Do you deliver outside Harare?

Yes — nationally, with transport priced per load. Erection is also covered nationally.

How big a lift can you do?

Kingson runs one telescopic mobile crane with a 25-tonne lifting capacity, used both on our own erection work and hired for other jobs.

Can I get a price without drawings?

Yes. Send site photos and a brief description, with the approximate span and height or opening dimensions. Include material and thickness if known. Where the job needs someone on site, a site visit is arranged within 2 working days, and a written quotation follows within 5 working days.
