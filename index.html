Engineering & Nesting Process

From DXF to first pierce.

Drawing intake

Engineering check

Manufacturability

Every part is checked for kerf allowance, minimum hole diameter against thickness, bend reliefs and the radii our press can actually hold. Problems get flagged here, not on the shop floor.
