How long does a quotation take?

Enquiries are acknowledged the same working day, and a written quotation follows within 5 working days. Where the job needs someone on site first, the site visit is arranged within 2 working days.

How long does a steel structure take?

A portal frame programme runs 6–10 weeks from order. A written quotation comes within 5 working days of your enquiry, and a site visit is arranged within 2 working days where the job needs one.

How do I get a price?

Send drawings or a description by WhatsApp on +263 772 262 869, or email admin1@kingsonengineering.co.zw. Technical drawings can go to technical@kingsonengineering.co.zw. Enquiries are acknowledged the same working day.

Where is Kingson Engineering based?

Kingson Engineering trades as Kingson Trading (Pvt) Ltd and works from one workshop at No. 1262 Tynwald Industries, Harare, Zimbabwe. It is open Monday to Saturday, 07:30 to 17:00, and enquiries reach Mr Murandu.

What areas do you cover?

Harare, and nationally across Zimbabwe. Erection is covered nationally and delivery outside Harare is priced per load.

Can I get a price without drawings?

Yes. Send site photos and a brief description, with the approximate span and height or opening dimensions. Include material and thickness if known. Where the job needs someone on site, a site visit is arranged within 2 working days, and a written quotation follows within 5 working days.

What can Kingson Engineering do?

Six things: structural steelwork, roof steelwork and trusses, fibre laser cutting, balustrades and roller shutter doors, general metal fabrication, and mobile cranage. All of it is fabricated at the Tynwald workshop and erected with Kingson’s own crane.
