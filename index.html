What we make

What we make

Four things, done in our own workshop and on site.

  • Structural steel

    Portal frames, columns, rafters and purlins — fabricated in the workshop and erected on site with our own crane.

    Send drawings, or the span, height and bay spacing you need.

  • Roof steel

    Trusses, purlins and sheeting for long-span roofs, new build or re-roof.

    Send the floor area, the pitch you want, and whether the walls are up.

  • Laser cutting

    Plate and sheet cut on a fiber laser, straight from your file.

    Send a DXF or DWG, the material and the thickness.

  • Cranage

    Mobile crane hire for lifting and placing steel, on our jobs or yours.

    Tell us the load, the site access and the date.
